Capitals

8 questions

What is the capital of Australia?

Sydney is the largest city, but Canberra was purpose-built as a compromise capital; Parliament moved there in 1927.

What is the capital of Türkiye?

Ankara became the capital in 1923 with the founding of the Republic. Istanbul is the largest city, not the capital.

What is the capital of Canada?

Queen Victoria chose Ottawa in 1857, partly for its safer distance from the US border.

Which country's official capital is Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte?

Colombo is Sri Lanka's commercial hub, but Parliament sits in nearby Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte.

What is the constitutional capital of Bolivia?

Sucre is the constitutional capital; La Paz is the seat of government, which is why both appear on Bolivia's card.

What is the capital of Nepal?

Kathmandu sits in a valley that also holds seven UNESCO World Heritage monument zones.

What is the capital of Switzerland?

Bern is the federal city and seat of government. Zürich is the largest city, Geneva the most international.

Which country has three capital cities?

Pretoria (executive), Cape Town (legislative) and Bloemfontein (judicial) split the roles.

Geography

8 questions

Which is the largest country by land area?

Russia covers about 17.1 million km² and 11 time zones. Canada is second.

Which country is the longest from north to south?

Chile stretches roughly 4,300 km from the Atacama to Patagonia while averaging under 180 km wide.

Which is the tallest free-standing mountain in the world?

Kilimanjaro rises 5,895 m on its own rather than as part of a range, which is what “free-standing” means.

Which lake is the deepest on Earth?

Baikal, in Siberia, reaches about 1,642 m and holds roughly a fifth of the world's unfrozen fresh water.

Which country has the only non-rectangular national flag?

Nepal's flag is two stacked pennants. Switzerland's and Vatican City's flags are square, which is still a rectangle.

Which country has the largest population?

India passed China in 2023 and now has around 1.43 billion people.

K2, the world's second-highest mountain, rises in which country's Karakoram range?

K2 (8,611 m) stands on the Pakistan–China border and is almost always climbed from the Pakistani side.

Which country has more lakes than the rest of the world combined?

Canada has roughly two million lakes; fresh water covers close to 9% of its territory.

Landmarks

6 questions

Machu Picchu is in which country?

The Inca citadel sits above the Urubamba valley in Peru, about 80 km from Cusco.

Angel Falls, the tallest waterfall on Earth, is in which country?

It drops 979 m from Auyán-tepui in Venezuela's Canaima National Park.

Borobudur, the world's largest Buddhist temple, is in which country?

Borobudur is a 9th-century temple in Central Java, Indonesia.

The cliffside Tiger's Nest monastery (Paro Taktsang) is in which country?

It clings to a cliff about 900 m above the Paro valley in Bhutan.

Salar de Uyuni, the largest salt flat, is in which country?

The salt flat covers about 10,500 km² of Bolivia's southwest and turns into a giant mirror after rain.

Hagia Sophia is in which country?

Built in Constantinople in 537 CE, it stands in modern Istanbul, Türkiye.

Culture & facts

6 questions

Which country is widely regarded as the birthplace of coffee?

The coffee plant is native to Ethiopia's Kaffa region; Brazil and Colombia became major growers much later.

Which was the first self-governing country to give women the vote, in 1893?

New Zealand led in 1893. Finland followed in 1906 as the first in Europe.

Which country abolished its army in 1948?

Costa Rica disbanded its military after its 1948 civil war and redirected the budget to education and health.

Which country has around 840 living languages, the most of any nation?

Papua New Guinea's rugged terrain kept communities isolated, so languages multiplied. Indonesia and Nigeria each have several hundred.

Which country measures progress with Gross National Happiness?

Bhutan coined the term in the 1970s and later wrote it into its constitution.

Which country skipped 30 December 2011 entirely?

Samoa moved west across the International Date Line to align its week with Australia and New Zealand, its main trading partners.

Where is the “Panama hat” actually made?

How this site works

Two files, no JavaScript, no external assets. It works offline and loads instantly.

Built from two files

Everything is index.html and style.css. Region filters, quiz tabs, answer feedback and the running score use radio inputs with :checked, :has() and CSS counters. Cards use content-visibility: auto, so off-screen cards skip layout and paint.

Adding a country

Copy any <article class="card">, set data-r to its region and fill in the fields. The “Showing N countries” count updates on its own.

Adding a question

Duplicate a <fieldset class="q">, give its radios a new shared name, and put class="opt ok" on the correct option. Numbering and scoring adjust automatically.

Worldbook. Populations are rounded 2024 estimates; facts are drawn from standard reference sources.
